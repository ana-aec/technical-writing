Project Overview

Legal and security requirements are often the biggest bottlenecks in release cycles. I developed this guide to act as an "Editorial Shield," empowering writers to make compliant decisions in real-time. By defining explicit data classification rules and standardization for redaction, I reduced the need for legal and security team intervention during documentation reviews by approximately 50%.

Editorial Philosophy

As a company with a global client base, we operate under strict regulatory requirements, including GDPR. This guide serves to protect our customers' confidentiality and our firm's intellectual property by enforcing a "Privacy by Design" approach to technical documentation.

Data Classification Categories

Technical authors must proactively scrub the following identifiers from all public-facing knowledge base articles, PDFs, and in-app content:

Category Restricted Examples
Corporate Registration numbers, office addresses, trade secrets, financial data, and IP addresses.
Individual Full names, government ID numbers, biometric/medical data, and sensitive demographics (race, religion, etc.).
